The Ikigai Onsen Village resort, with 60% of its area given to public natural landscape: a stone-and-water plaza, a bamboo park, a retention lake and land-art grass hills. The project includes a 5-star hotel, onsen villas, a premium onsen area, a four-season pool, a pedestrian street and an Asian–European restaurant.

Delivered at Raymond Architectural Design Office.
