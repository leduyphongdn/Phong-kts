Onsen

Yoko Onsen Quang Hanh

Discipline
Onsen
Location
Quang Hanh, Quảng Ninh
images
34

Nestled among green mountain slopes, this is the first Japanese-standard hot-spring resort in Vietnam. The complex has onsen bathing, therapy, a spa, traditional Japanese-style lodging and an organic restaurant. The spring water is about 60 to 70 °C and can be enjoyed year-round.

Delivered at Raymond Architectural Design Office.
