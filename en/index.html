About

Architect Lê Duy Phong

Head of Design · Chief of Da Nang Office – Raymond Architectural Design Office

An architect pursuing the spirit of Japanese design: minimal, respectful of nature, and attentive to every detail.

I am currently Head of Design and Chief of the Da Nang Office at Raymond Architectural Design Office. My work spans resorts, onsen, architecture, planning, interior and landscape projects across Vietnam.

To me, a good building begins by listening to the terrain, the climate and the people who will live in it. I stay with a project from the first idea until the building is complete.
